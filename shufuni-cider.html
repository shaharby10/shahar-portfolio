SHUFUNI
CIDER

Description

Brand Identity, Art Direction, Packaging Design

2025

Academic · Bezalel

Shufuni is an Israeli alcoholic cider brand featuring four unconventional locally grown fruits: pitanga, lychee, loquat, and sabra cactus. Blending local culture, art, and lifestyle, the brand uses a bold, playful visual language that gives each flavor its own distinct personality. Each bottle is designed as a collectible piece of art, part of a brand world made to be seen, held, and shown off.

The name Shufuni, derived from Arabic for “look at me,” sets the brand’s playful, showy tone. Its visual identity draws inspiration from Jaffa’s arches, collage, vintage Israeli book covers, and the work of Yayoi Kusama.

Each fruit has its own color palette and texture, combining AI-generated fruit illustrations with hand-drawn, digitally colored patterns. Together, they create a distinctive visual language that brings four different flavors into one cohesive identity.

Shufuni was designed to be more than a drink, but part of a lifestyle: sunshine, friends, bold colors, and a little showing off. More Friday afternoon than Friday night.

I brought this world to life by leading the entire production, from assembling the bottles and casting to styling, photography, and post-production. The imagery captures the brand as a natural part of everyday moments, rather than a traditional alcohol campaign.
