THE COST OF
CONSUMPTION

Description

Illustration, Motion Design

2024

Academic · Bezalel

“The Cost of Consumption” is a series of three looping animations created as animated headers for episodes of the Israeli podcast “The Consumer Crisis”, exploring consumer culture, the fashion industry, and the world of work. Each animation translates an episode’s theme into an illustrated scene within a shared visual language. Drawn in black ink, the illustrations were digitally colored using handmade marks, scanned and incorporated as textures.

EPISODE 05 — IT’S TIME TO WORK LESS

EPISODE 03 — THE DIRTY LAUNDRY OF THE FASHION INDUSTRY

EPISODE 04 — RECYCLING WON’T SAVE US
